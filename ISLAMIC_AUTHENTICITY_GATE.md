# Islamic Education Authenticity Gate — ٢٠٢٦–٢٠٢٧

## Purpose
Build Islamic education that forms the learner rather than merely supplying religious information. Authenticity means fidelity to the Qur'an, reliably authenticated Sunnah, sound Islamic principles, and the educational objectives of the supplied curriculum—without inventing religious claims or attributing statements to Allah or His Messenger ﷺ without evidence.

## Non-negotiable source hierarchy
1. Qur'an: exact verse, surah, and verified wording.
2. Authentic Sunnah: hadith whose grading is established by reliable hadith scholarship; source and grading must be recorded when used as evidence.
3. Established principles of Islamic creed, worship, ethics, and jurisprudence appropriate to the learner's age.
4. Official Palestinian curriculum/source documents when they define the learning requirement.
5. Teacher-created examples and activities only as pedagogical applications, never as religious evidence.

## Truth controls
- No fabricated hadith, quotation, story, miracle, ruling, attribution, or scholarly consensus.
- Do not use a weak or disputed report as a factual religious proof without explicitly marking its status and educational reason.
- Distinguish revelation from interpretation, scholarly opinion, historical narration, and teacher explanation.
- Never convert a pedagogical simplification into a theological claim.
- Preserve the original competency wording before normalization.

## Formation model
`KNOW → UNDERSTAND → BELIEVE → PRACTISE → CHARACTER → RESPONSIBILITY`

A strong Islamic learning outcome should, where appropriate, move from knowledge to observable conduct: truthful speech, prayer and worship correctly, mercy, justice, respect for parents, keeping trusts, cleanliness, cooperation, responsibility, restraint from harm, care for people and creation, and constructive citizenship.

## Age-appropriateness
- Grades 1–2: concrete experiences, short authentic texts, modelling, practice, repetition, love of Allah and His Messenger ﷺ, good manners and worship routines.
- Grades 3–4: stronger explanation, evidence recognition, simple reasoning, distinction between correct and incorrect practice, and responsible application in real situations.

## Assessment integrity
Assessment must measure the intended competency, not memorization alone. Evidence may include explanation, demonstration, application, decision-making, conduct in a simulated situation, or accurate recall where recall itself is the intended outcome.

## Traceability
Every Islamic lesson or assessment must preserve:
`CURRICULUM SOURCE → ISLAMIC EVIDENCE → COMPETENCY → LEARNING OUTCOME → OBSERVABLE PERFORMANCE → ACTIVITY → EVIDENCE → ASSESSMENT`

## Final gate
An Islamic educational artifact is NO-GO if it contains an unverified religious attribution, changes the meaning of a Qur'anic verse or authentic hadith, presents opinion as revelation, teaches a ruling beyond the source/evidence available, or reduces Islam to information without the intended formative outcome.
